\subsection*{\deva{सप्तदश चौपाई} \(Seventeenth Chaupai\)}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{\deva{सप्तदश चौपाई} \(Seventeenth Chaupai\) — \deva{तुम्हरो मंत्र...}}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.55\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}

{\large \linespread{1.5}\selectfont
\deva{तुम्हरो मंत्र बिभीषन माना।}\\
\deva{लंकेस्वर भए सब जग जाना॥}
\par}

\vspace{0.5em}

{\large \linespread{1.5}\selectfont
\telu{తుమ్హరో మంత్ర బిభీషన మానా।}\\
\telu{లంకేశ్వర భఏ సబ జగ జానా॥}
\par}

\vspace{0.5em}

{\large \linespread{1.3}\selectfont
\textit{tumharo maṃtra bibhīṣana mānā |}\\
\textit{laṃkesvara bhae saba jaga jānā ||}
\par}
\end{minipage}%
\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.42\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\begin{tcolorbox}[anchorbox]
\textbf{The Power of Good Counsel:} Vibhishana was surrounded by a toxic, negative environment in Lanka, but he chose to listen to Hanuman's wise advice (\textit{Mantra}), which ultimately saved his life and made him king. Success heavily depends on seeking out and listening to positive, wise mentors.
\vspace{0.5em}\newline A brilliant, logical argument (Mantra) from a wise mentor can save your reputation and elevate your career.\newline\null\hfill\href{https://www.valmiki.iitk.ac.in/sloka?field_kanda_tid=6&language=dv&field_sarga_value=17}{\textit{Yuddha Kanda, 17:50-68}}
\end{tcolorbox}
\end{minipage}

\textbf{ \deva{सरल-संस्कृतम्}:}\\
 \deva{भवतः मन्त्रं (परामर्शं / उपदेशं) विभीषणः अङ्गीकृतवान्।}\\
 \deva{तेन सः लङ्केश्वरः (लङ्कायाः राजा) अभवत् इति सर्वं जगत् जानाति।} \\

Vibhishana heeded your counsel. 
By doing so, he became the King of Lanka, a fact the entire world knows.
