विंशतितम चौपाई \(Twentieth Chaupai\)

दुर्गम काज जगत के जेते।
सुगम अनुग्रह तुम्हरे तेते॥
దుర్గమ కాజ జగత కే జేతే।
సుగమ అనుగ్రహ తుమ్హరే తేతే॥
durgama kāja jagata ke jete |
sugama anugraha tumhare tete ||
सरल-संस्कृतम्:
जगतः यावन्ति अपि दुर्गमाणि (कठिनानि) कार्याणि सन्ति
तानि सर्वाणि भवतः अनुग्रहेण (कृपया) सुगमानि भवन्ति॥
Whatever difficult tasks exist in the world
they all become easily attainable by your grace.

The Ultimate Problem Solver

He takes 'impossible' tasks (Durgam) and makes them manageable (Sugam). When students face overwhelming exams, massive projects, or complex challenges, remembering his laser-like focus and unmatched work ethic provides a blueprint for making the impossible solvable.

💡

Linguistic Notes

  • Aspirated Consonants (म्ह and न्ह): In words like तुम्हरे (Tumhare) and कीन्हा (Keenhaa), the `h` merges with `m` and `n` to form a single aspirated sound. In classical Awadhi meter, this does not make the preceding short vowel heavy. Therefore, तु (tu) in Tumhare remains exactly 1 beat!
