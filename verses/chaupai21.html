एकविंशतितम चौपाई \(Twenty-First Chaupai\)

राम दुआरे तुम रखवारे।
होत न आज्ञा बिनु पैसारे॥
రామ దుఆరే తుమ రఖవారే।
హోత న ఆజ్ఞా బిను పైసారే॥
rāma duāre tuma rakhavāre |
hota na ājñā binu paisāre ||
सरल-संस्कृतम्:
श्रीरामस्य द्वारे भवान् एव रक्षकः (द्वारपालः) अस्ति।
भवतः आज्ञां विना तत्र प्रवेशः न भवति॥
At the door of Lord Rama, you are the protector/guard.
Without your permission, entry is not possible.

The Gatekeeper of Discipline

He guards the door to success (Ram Duaare Tum Rakhvare). Nothing worthwhile in life is achieved without passing through the gates of discipline. Hanuman represents the hard work, routine, and discipline required before anyone can attain mastery or peace.
