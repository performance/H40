त्रयोविंशतितम चौपाई \(Twenty-Third Chaupai\)

आपन तेज सम्हारो आपै।
तीनों लोक हाँक तें काँपै॥
ఆపన తేజ సమ్హారో ఆపై।
తీనోం లోక హాఁక తేఁ కాఁపై॥
āpana teja samhāro āpai |
tīnõ loka hā̃ka tẽ kā̃pai ||
सरल-संस्कृतम्:
भवतः प्रचण्डं तेजः भवान् स्वयम् एव सम्भालितुं शक्नोति।
भवतः एकया गर्जनया (हुङ्कारेण) त्रयः लोकाः कम्पन्ते॥
Only you yourself can control your own immense splendor and power.
At your single roar, all three worlds tremble.

Self-Regulation

He possessed immense, earth-shaking energy and talent, but he kept it under perfect control (Samharo Aapai). Raw intelligence, talent, and energy are completely useless without the self-discipline to regulate and direct them properly.
