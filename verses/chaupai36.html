षट्त्रिंशत्तम चौपाई (Thirty-Sixth Chaupai)

संकट कटै मिटै सब पीरा।
जो सुमिरै हनुमत बलबीरा॥
సంకట కటై మిటై సబ పీరా।
జో సుమిరై హనుమత బలబీరా॥
saṅkaṭa kaṭai miṭai saba pīrā |
jo sumirai hanumata balabīrā ||
सरल-संस्कृतम्:
तस्य सर्वाणि सङ्कटानि छिद्यन्ते, सर्वाः पीडाः च नश्यन्ति
यः बलवीरं (महाबल-शालिनं) हनूमन्तं स्मरति॥
All crises are cut away, and all pain is erased for whoever remembers the mighty and brave Hanuman.

Converting Constraints to Opportunities

In the *Valmiki Ramayana* (*Sundara Kanda, 48:45-50*), when bound by Indrajit's weapon, Hanuman intentionally chose not to break free immediately. He realized that being bound allowed him to be taken directly to Ravana's court, giving him the perfect opportunity to meet the enemy king face-to-face and deliver a message of peace and warning. It teaches us to convert constraints into opportunities.
