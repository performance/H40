सप्तत्रिंशत्तम चौपाई (Thirty-Seventh Chaupai)

जै जै जै हनुमान गोसाईं।
कृपा करहु गुरु देव की नाईं॥
జై జై జై హనుమాన గోసాయీఁ।
కృపా కరహు గురు దేవ కీ నాయీఁ॥
jai jai jai hanumāna gosāī̃ |
kṛpā karahu guru deva kī nāī̃ ||
सरल-संस्कृतम्:
हे गोस्वामिन् (इन्द्रियाणां स्वामिन्) हनुमन्! भवतः जयः, जयः, जयः अस्तु।
भवान् मम उपरि इष्ट-गुरुदेवः इव कृपां करोतु॥
Hail, Hail, All Hail to you, Lord Hanuman, Master of the Senses!
Please bestow your grace upon me, just like a Supreme Guru.

The Ultimate Mentor

By accepting a true guide (Gurudeva), we gain the strength to discover our potential. In the *Valmiki Ramayana* (*Kishkindha Kanda, 66:31-35*), when Hanuman was silent with self-doubt, his mentor Jambavan reminded him of his divine origins and unmatched capabilities. A true mentor wipes away doubt and inspires us to take on challenges.
