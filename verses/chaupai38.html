अष्टत्रिंशत्तम चौपाई (Thirty-Eighth Chaupai)

जो सत बार पाठ कर कोई।
छूटहि बंदि महा सुख होई॥
జో సత బార పాఠ కర కోఈ।
ఛూటహి బంది మహా సుఖ హోఈ॥
jo sata bāra pāṭha kara koī |
chūṭahi bandi mahā sukha hoī ||
सरल-संस्कृतम्:
यः कश्चित् शत-वारम् अस्य पाठं करोति
सः बन्धनात् मुच्यते, तस्य महान् सुखं च भवति॥
Whoever recites this one hundred times is freed from all bondage and experiences immense happiness.

The Power of Persistence

Sata Baar (100 times) represents unwavering consistency. In the *Valmiki Ramayana* (*Sundara Kanda, 12:10*), when Hanuman initially fails to find Sita and feels discouraged, he recovers his resolve by declaring: *Anirvedah shriyo mulam* ("Persistence/cheerfulness is the root of all success and prosperity"). He renews his search, proving that mastery and liberation from failure are only built through relentless, repetitive effort.

Linguistic Notes

  • The श/ष → स Shift: Yet another perfect example of palatal softening! The strict Sanskrit शत (Shata/100) becomes the pure, flowing Awadhi सत (Sata).
